## Required next audit (not yet done)

For each instruction format, transcribe reference-backed opcode and extension-field assignments; expand operand/qualifying-predicate profiles; classify normal, ignored, unconditional-reserved, conditionally-reserved, and processor-generation-dependent cases. Compare each legal encoding against production translation, then add real execution and architectural exception tests.

A generic fallback may be the **correct** implementation of a reserved encoding, or it may mask multiple legal instructions. An implemented path may be numerically or architecturally wrong. Do not treat source grep, fallback-site counts, or successful assembly as proof of ISA completeness.

Dynamic `unimpl_msg` calls belong to the break/callgate helper and are tracked separately, rather than assigned to A/I/M/B/X.
The F unit already has a separate positive-family audit; its remaining generic fallback is for non-positive/unadjudicated encodings, not evidence of a missing named family.
